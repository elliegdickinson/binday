# CLAUDE.md — Bin Day brand rules

When building or editing the Bin Day website, treat these brand rules as hard constraints.

## Brand intent
Bin Day is a simple public-utility-style product that makes bin collections easy to understand and sync to a calendar.
It should feel useful first, fun second: clear, bold, accessible, slightly cheeky.

## Typography
- Display/headlines: `Space Grotesk`, 700–800.
- Body/UI: `Atkinson Hyperlegible`, 400/700.
- Fallback: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
- Do not replace with rounded, bubbly, or generic SaaS fonts.

## Colours
- Green: #C8FF38
- Blue: #456CFF
- Cream: #F7F4EA
- Ink: #161616
- White: #FFFFFF
- Grey: #6B6B6B

Collection colours:
- General: #454545
- Recycling: #456CFF
- Garden: #4B9E39
- Food: #FF7A2F
- Paper/card: #A23CF0

Never rely on colour alone; always pair collection colour with icon + text label.

## UI style
- Cream page background by default.
- Black/ink text.
- Large Space Grotesk headings.
- 12–16px card/button radii.
- No glassmorphism.
- No pastel SaaS gradients.
- No excessive pill shapes.
- No decorative 3D illustration unless explicitly requested.
- No account wall for the main postcode → collections → calendar flow.
- Buttons should be obvious and at least 48px high.

## Copy
Preferred:
- What bin is it this week?
- No account. No faff.
- Here’s what’s going out.
- Never think about bin day again.
- Add bin days to my calendar
- That’s the bins sorted.
- Your bins. Your calendar. Sorted.

Avoid forced bin puns.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
Use semantic HTML, strong focus states, labelled form controls, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and 16px+ body copy.

## Brand assets
Use files in `/assets/logo/`.
Design tokens are in `/tokens/brand.css` and `/tokens/brand-tokens.json`.

Do not reinterpret the brand into a generic SaaS product.
