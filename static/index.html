Help

Why isn't my council here?

Some councils need a property id we can't work out from a postcode alone, so they need a bit of plumbing writing first. They're listed under "Not supported yet" so you can see where yours stands.

What's a UPRN and why do you want mine?

It's the unique number for your property. Most councils key their bin lookup on it, and there's no free national way to go from a postcode to one - so for some councils we have to ask. You can find yours at findmyaddress.co.uk.

Is this official?

No. The dates come from your council's own website, but this isn't run by them. If something looks wrong, believe your council, not us.

Do you store my address?

No. There's no account and no database. The calendar link itself carries your property id, which is how it keeps working.
