# Social assets

Generated by [`social/build_social.py`](../social/build_social.py). Run it from
the repo root; the brand fonts download to `social/fonts/` on first run.

```bash
./.venv/bin/python social/build_social.py
```

## Purpose

These graphics exist to be dropped into a community group or a feed by someone
who is not a designer, and to be understood in about a second while scrolling.
So: one message per graphic, no explaining, no feature list.

## Hierarchy

Strict, top to bottom, and never reordered:

1. **Logo**, top left, small. It is a signature, not the message.
2. **Headline**, dominant - the largest thing on the canvas by a clear margin.
3. **One explanatory line**, two lines of body at most.
4. **One real collection result** on a white card. Relative timing
   (`TOMORROW`) is the biggest thing on the card, in the recycling blue, with
   the brand rays beside it. The bin name sits above in grey, the real date
   below in ink.
5. **CTA**: a lime pill, with the bare domain smaller underneath.
6. **Sign-off**: `Your bins. Your calendar. Sorted.` in grey, deliberately quiet.

The bin crops off the bottom-right corner, oversized, behind everything.
It never loses its wheel to the crop - a beheaded bin reads as a bug.

## Preferred headlines

- `NEVER MISS / BIN DAY AGAIN.` (default)
- `WHAT BIN IS IT / THIS WEEK?`
- `HERE'S WHAT'S / GOING OUT.`

Two lines, all caps, Space Grotesk Bold, full stop on the second line. Bold is
the heaviest cut of the variable font - there is no 800 to select.

## CTA treatment

Lime pill, ink text, ink outline, arrow after the words. The domain goes
**underneath in small bold ink**, not inside the pill: inside, it competes with
the verb and both shrink.

## Formats

| File | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `social/bin-day-square.png` | 1080 × 1080 | main feed graphic |
| `social/bin-day-portrait.png` | 1080 × 1350 | portrait feed option |
| `social/bin-day-story.png` | 1080 × 1920 | stories / reels |
| `static/assets/social/bin-day-og.png` | 1200 × 630 | link preview, served as `og:image` |

Each format re-composes at its own scale rather than being resized: the story
carries extra top padding so the block clears the platform's own UI, and the
portrait needs a larger bin or the lower third goes dead.

## Do not

- Do not put more than one message on a graphic.
- Do not add a feature list, screenshots of the site, or a QR code.
- Do not shrink the headline to make room for anything.
- Do not use a fake or rounded collection date - the card shows a real result.
- Do not recolour the bin, add gradients, shadows or glass.
- Do not add bin puns beyond the ones in `CLAUDE.md`.
- Do not crop the bin past its wheel.
- Do not stretch one render to another aspect ratio.
